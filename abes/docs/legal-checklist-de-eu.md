# DE / EU Legal Checklist (Mandatory before Launch)

This checklist must be completed and confirmed by a human before any public launch.

## Basic Requirements (Germany / EU)

- [ ] Impressum (full name, address, email, if applicable VAT-ID / register) – DDG
- [ ] Datenschutzerklärung (GDPR compliant)
- [ ] Cookie Consent (if non-essential cookies / tracking)
- [ ] AGB (if applicable)
- [ ] Widerrufsbelehrung (14 days) + Widerrufsbutton (from 19 June 2026)
- [ ] Correct price display (PAngV)
- [ ] Information about delivery times, payment methods, restrictions

## Digital Products / Services

- [ ] Conformity and update obligations considered (BGB digital content rules)
- [ ] Clear description of what the product actually delivers (Truth Layer)

## E-Commerce / Physical Products

- [ ] Shipping & returns process defined
- [ ] Product safety if applicable

## Business Form

- [ ] Decision: Kleinunternehmer or regular VAT
- [ ] Gewerbeanmeldung if required
- [ ] Tax considerations noted
